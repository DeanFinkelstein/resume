# Adopting AI safely in a Salesforce shop

**What it is.** A one-page briefing for an IT lead deciding whether and how to let a team use Claude alongside Salesforce. It answers three questions in order: who can log in, where the data goes, what it costs. Then it gives the seven-step rollout I would run.

**Why it exists.** A client's IT team needed exactly this before they would approve a pilot: OAuth, data retention, and licensing, in one page, in words they could forward to their own leadership. The version they got became a reusable template at my firm. This is the public version, rebuilt from current sources with nothing client-specific in it.

**What it proves.** That the security and licensing conversation can be had in plain words without losing precision, and that the facts were checked against primary sources on a stated date rather than remembered.

**What goes in.** The current terms from Anthropic and Salesforce (links and check date at the bottom of the briefing).

**What comes out.** `BRIEFING.md` (source of truth) and `BRIEFING.pdf` (the one page you forward).

**Time.** One afternoon, most of it reading the policies rather than writing.

**What I would change next time.** Add a one-line "what changed since last quarter" box at the top and keep it current. Two of the facts in this briefing changed during 2026 alone: the Covered Models retention rule and the masking status for agents.

Source tag for the resume card: facts checked against eight primary sources on 2026-10-17, listed in the briefing.
